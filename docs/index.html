How it works

Data. nflverse play-by-play (every snap since 2012), schedules with closing lines since 1999, weekly player stats and injury reports. Live lines and props from The Odds API across US sportsbooks.

Team model. Opponent-adjusted offense and defense EPA per play and points, weighted toward recent games and carried across seasons; the projected starting QB's own EPA; QB changes, rest, division games, roof, wind and cold. Two ridge regressions — one for margin, one for total — retrained every week on 14 seasons, with recent seasons weighted more.

Honest read. Tested season by season on games it never saw, the model is less accurate than the closing line and its picks against the spread land around 48–50%. Closing lines already contain everything public data can tell you. Model leans are shown as context and tracked live, but the tool's edge is meant to come from best-price shopping, early-number CLV and softer prop markets.
